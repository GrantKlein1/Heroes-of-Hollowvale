The files in gfx/ are the original pack. atlas.json records source rectangles
measured from those sheets (objects trimmed to opaque bounds).

Files in authored/ are derivatives made for Heroes of Hollowvale (dirt and
cobble recolors, a timber cottage, a stone well, camp props, and 16px
enemies). They are also released under CC0.
