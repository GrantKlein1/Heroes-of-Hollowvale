gfx/ is the pack. atlas.json lists source rectangles measured from those sheets
(objects trimmed to their opaque bounds). authored/ holds tiles derived from
those rectangles: a dirt recolor of the sand path, a one-row river composited
from the water edges, a timber cottage recolor of the barn, and small sprites
drawn in the same palette where the pack has no equivalent (well, bandits,
goblins, tent, campfire).
