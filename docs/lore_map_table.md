Source: `newLore_expanded.txt` (the Keeper’s telling of year 1477). `newLore.txt` is unchanged and is not the source of these line numbers.

Confidence: **high** = the quote states a direction, a distance, or both. **medium** = the place is named and seated in a country, but the quote does not give a full bearing and a distance. **low** = only a loose association.

## Remaining gaps

- **The Unmarched East**, past Sable Crossing, is named and pointed east, and the Keeper refuses a distance. It is the edge of the map on purpose.
- **North of Lake Merrow** and **south of the eight-day depth of the Wastes** are not given a further coast, pass, or people. No southern sea is stated.
- **The Ash Road’s full walking-time from Mourngate to the monoliths** is not totaled. The stages are placed (Mourngate, Blackspire’s country, Fallowridge spur, monoliths, Moonshadow, the veil), and the ash pace is five leagues a day. A single figure for the whole ruined line is not.
- **Thurnok’s Prison** and the sealed **Heart Chamber** are placed (under the Stonehold gate; a day’s descent under the Halls) without a counted stair-depth in fathoms.
- **The true Sword of Aeltharion** is still unlocated on purpose. Starfall is a glimpse, the Cavern is a whisper, the Cinder Vault may hold only the Star-Shard Splinter.
- **Whether Durvak Stonevein still lives** in the Deep, and whether the Keeper is one undying host or an office, are left as the tapside tells them.
- **The inside of the Gates of Flame** is undescribed past the eastern cliffs of Gate Isle. They are shut.
