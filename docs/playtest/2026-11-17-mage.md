# Mage playtest — 2026-11-17

Route: `/?qa=1&seed=1` on `f2088ad` (main is past `09f6eca`). No `renderer` or `maps` query. Blend sprites and Tiled maps are the only scene path, and that is enough for this pass. The spell wheel does not depend on the removed opt-outs.

Played headlessly by `tests/e2e/playtest-mage.spec.ts` (system Chrome; the Playwright Chromium download failed). Screenshots are in `docs/playtest/mage/`.

## Steps

1. Title screen, Start Game.
2. Second Choose button (Mage). Lands in Brackenford (`village`).
3. V opens inventory. Spellbook is in the first storage slot. Apprentice Staff is hotbar 1, Mana Potion ×2 is hotbar 2, Emerald / Sapphire / Ruby ×2 sit in 7–9. Cloth robes are in the chest slot.
4. Pointer-down on the spellbook, move onto empty hotbar slot 3, pointer-up.
5. Click that same slot, close inventory with V, press 3 so the book is the selected item.
6. Hold K. The wheel shows Lightning Bolt [1] on top, Blink [2] on the right, Life Leech [3] on the bottom, Mana Shield [4] on the left. Press 1.
7. Release K. Press Space once.

## What worked

- Class select, Brackenford, and the starting loadout match the mage seed.
- Holding K with the spellbook selected opens the wheel. The four labels and `[1]`–`[4]` match the keyboard map in `Game.jsx` (1 lightning, 2 blink, 3 leech, 4 shield). Pressing 1 sets `session.spellId` to `lightning` and shows "Selected Lightning Bolt".
- One Space press plays `mage_cast_lightning_v2_down` and spawns one projectile: texture `spell_fx_v2`, fly clip `lightning_bolt_v2`, damage **15**.
- No page errors and no console errors on this pass. `fxLayer.js` did not throw. The bolt is a Phaser sprite, not a DOM image, so the DOM pool stayed empty.

## Bugs (reported, not fixed)

### Drag-and-drop does not drop the spellbook

A real drag (pointer down, move, pointer up) picks the book up and leaves it on the cursor. After pointer-up the storage slot is empty, the hotbar slot is still empty, and the drag ghost is still showing (`storage: 0, hotbar: 0, ghost: 1`). The inventory only places a stack on a later pointer-down. A click on hotbar slot 3 then puts the book there.

The footer says "Left-click: drag/swap/merge". There is no pointer-up handler on the slots. Closing the inventory while the ghost is up would put the book back, because `returnDraggedToInventory` runs on close.

Not fixed. Inventory lives in `Game.jsx`, which this package must not edit.

### Lightning does not spend mana

Mana was 100 before Space and 100 after the bolt. No "Not enough mana." notice.

The blend attack hook in `Player.js` handles mage + spellbook by calling `Player.cast`, which arms the v2 lightning clip and never subtracts mana. `HollowvaleScene._tryAttack` still has the 15 mana gate and the `mana -= 15` spend, and that branch does not run for this cast.

Not fixed. That path is outside `fxLayer.js`. Spell damage was not changed: the spawned bolt's damage field is still 15, and `spells.js` is untouched.

### Class card lists spells the wheel does not cast

The Mage card still says `[Z] Firebolt`, `[X] Frost Nova`, and `[C] Arcane Shield`. The wheel that actually opens is lightning, blink, leech, and shield, cast with Space while the spellbook is selected.

Not fixed. The card copy is in `Game.jsx`.

## fxLayer

No null camera, no bad comment, and no thrown error showed up while the wheel was open or during the cast. `client/src/game/fxLayer.js` is unchanged.

## Damage

Lightning bolt damage observed at cast time: 15. `client/src/game/combat/spells.js` and `client/src/game/combat/balance.js` were not edited.
