## Findings (not fixed)

These are outside `dodge.js`. This package does not change them.

1. **The vanish clip plays while the sprite is forced off.** `HollowvaleScene._present` hides the player for the whole `thiefVanishUntil` second, which starts on the same J press that calls `Player.vanish()`. The sampled anim was `thief_vanish_up` with `visible: false` and `vanishState.invisible` still false. The disappear clip is not something you can see. `Player.reappear()` exists and is not called from the scene. After the swing, visibility drops again until the smoke timer ends (`showSprite` is true during smoke only while `_attacking`).
2. **The Thief card lists different controls than the HUD.** The card abilities are `[Z] Backstab (bonus rear damage)`, `[X] Smoke Bomb (brief invisibility)`, and `[C] Pick Lock (open locks)`. The in-game hint is Attack: Space and Invisibility: J, with WASD, E, V, and F2 along the top. The card copy is in `Game.jsx`, which this package must not edit.

Dodge cooldown and travel distance were already consistent with the atlas (`thief_dodge.json` i-frames `[1, 4]`, `travelPx` 48) and with `dodge.test.js`. No null crash showed up on this route, so no guard was added.
