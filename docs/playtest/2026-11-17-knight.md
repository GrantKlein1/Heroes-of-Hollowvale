## Fixed

`SCENE_HINTS` in `client/src/game/sceneCatalog.js` still had a `path` key, and the dungeon hint said `bottom-left = Path`. There is no path scene. `enterScene` with an unknown id warns and stays put. The `path` key is removed. The dungeon hint is now `Dungeon entrance. E: bottom-right = Wilderness`, which matches `exit_wilderness` on the cavern mouth. This playtest did not enter the cavern, so that hint was not on screen. The village and wilderness hints that were on screen do not name a path.

## Bugs not fixed

These are outside `SCENE_HINTS`.

1. **Tharos's talk rect is behind the bar.** Walking to the counter stops on the stools. Measured body at the stool: `{x: 349, y: 196, w: 23, h: 7}`. The talk rect is `{x: 320, y: 40, w: 64, h: 96}` (`Press E to talk to Tharos`). They do not overlap, and the active prompt was empty. See `06-bar-approach.png`. The panel does open if the body is placed inside that rect and E is pressed: heading "Tharos the Bartender", the dragon and Hollowvale topic buttons, and Leave. See `07-bartender.png`.

2. **The Flagon's exit mat covers the dining floor.** `exit_village` in `tavern.tmj` is 128×128 at (256, 224) on a 640×384 room, and the tavern spawn sits inside it. Standing among the tables shows `Press E to exit to Brackenford` (`05-tavern.png`). The hint still says to press E near the bottom. That sentence was left as-is; it is not a typo.

3. **Class cards list keys the game does not use.** The knight card on the class screen says `[Z] Shield Bash`, `[X] Defensive Stance`, and `[C] Power Strike` (`02-class-select.png`). In `HollowvaleScene`, Shift dodges, C held is the knight's block, and Space attacks. Z and X are not read there. `Game.jsx` is outside this package.
