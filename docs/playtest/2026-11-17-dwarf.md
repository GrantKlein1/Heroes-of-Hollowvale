# Playtest: dwarf axe swing in Brackenford

Run date: 2026-11-18. Base `5e92601` (`origin/main`), which includes `09f6eca` (painted renderer and `?renderer` / `?maps` opt-outs removed). Route `/?qa=1&seed=1`. Viewport 1280×720. System Chrome, because the Playwright Chromium download failed (`ECONNRESET` from `cdn.playwright.dev`).

Ale was not selected and no consume key was pressed. The cavern pickaxe was not used.

## Steps

1. Title screen, Start Game.
2. Fourth Choose button. That card is the dwarf (`classId` `dwarf`).
3. Brackenford (`scene()` `village`). Walk north with the up arrow.
4. Space, facing up, held until attack frame 3 (the atlas hit frame).
5. Step east, Space again, held until the right-facing hit frame.

## What happened

The swing plays. Space on the dwarf with the battle axe in hotbar slot 1 switches the texture to `dwarf_attack` and enters `ATTACK`.

| Moment | Anim | Frame | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idle at the south entrance | `dwarf_idle_down` | `dwarf_idle_down` | (319, 345.6) |
| After walking north | `dwarf_idle_up` | `dwarf_idle_up` | (319, 286.9) |
| Space, facing up | `dwarf_attack_up` | `dwarf_attack_up_3` | (319, 286.9) |
| Space, facing right | `dwarf_attack_right` | `dwarf_attack_right_3` | (361.7, 286.9) |

Frame 3 is the chop. Facing right, the axe head is extended in front of the dwarf. Facing up, the same hit frame is the back of the overhead chop, which matches the attack sheet note ("chop into a forward crouch"). No page errors. No console errors. No HTTP 404s.

`resolveAnimWithFallback` is not on this path. The dwarf swing is `Player.attack` → `dwarf_attack_<dir>`. Brackenford had no enemy alert, so `fallbacks.js` was not entered. There was no null crash to guard. `fallbacks.js` is unchanged.

## Findings (not fixed)

These are outside `client/src/game/combat/fallbacks.js`. This package does not change them.

1. **South spawn sits in the wilderness exit.** The dwarf appears at the south gate already inside the exit beacon. The "Wilderness" label and the hotbar cover the idle sprite (`03-brackenford-idle.png`). Walking north clears the sprite (`04-brackenford-walk.png`). Moving that spawn would be a map or scene change, not a null guard in `fallbacks.js`.
2. **The class sheet clips the dwarf card at 1280×720.** The fourth Choose button, the portrait, and the start of the description are on screen. Strengths, abilities, and the starting items (battle axe, armor, pickaxe, ale) sit below the fold (`02-class-select.png`). The modal can scroll. The class sheet is `Game.jsx`, which this package does not edit.
3. **Headless music warnings only.** Chrome logged `[Audio] Failed to load` for `/audio/introAndVillageMusic.mp3`, `/audio/backgroundMusic1.mp3`, and `/audio/fightMusic1.mp3`. Those files are present under `client/public/audio/` and none of the responses were 404. WebGL also logged `GPU stall due to ReadPixels`. Both are this VM's headless Chrome, not a missing clip.
